# Adopt ONDTF

This is the operational entry point for organisations using ONDTF to construct or govern a Digital Trust Framework. It reuses existing ONDTF material rather than introducing a parallel method.

## 1. Decide whether ONDTF fits

Use ONDTF when the problem requires a governed relationship between identity, authority, policy, evidence, assurance, decision, effect, accountability and remedy across multiple institutional or technical actors. Do not use it merely to choose a credential format, identity provider, ledger, registry product or software stack.

## 2. Bound the scope

Record the jurisdiction/governing domain, sector/public purpose, trust services and consequential effects, affected parties, governing/supervisory authorities, required interoperability or recognition relationships, and explicit exclusions.

## 3. Run Guided Framework Construction

Use [Guided Framework Construction](guided-framework-construction.md), [Construction Stages](construction-stages.md), [Decision States and Review Gates](decision-states-and-review-gates.md), and [Contradiction and Completeness](contradiction-and-completeness.md). Establish capability/governance needs before technology selection.

## 4. Produce the profile package

Use [Generated Artefacts](generated-artefacts.md). The [starter package](https://github.com/qbf-consulting/open-national-digital-trust-framework/tree/main/starter) provides a small working skeleton. Replacing placeholders does not confer conformance, legal authority, approval or certification.

## 5. Implement against observable requirements

Use the stable [Requirements Register](../core-specification/requirements-register.md), applicable conformance class and selected profile. TSMM, TIS or alternatives may be selected; none is mandatory.

## 6. Collect evidence while implementing

Record authority, decisions, lifecycle state, assurance evidence, status/freshness, incidents, exceptions, challenge/remedy and change history as implementation proceeds. Treat clarification requests as evidence of implementability rather than silently resolving them through maintainer knowledge.

## 7. Make bounded conformance claims

A claim must identify ONDTF version, conformance class, profile, scope, evidence and assessment authority. Unqualified “ONDTF compliant” is not a valid claim.

## 8. Maintain the framework

Apply controlled change, evidence invalidation, reassessment, suspension/revocation, incident handling, appeal and remedy as ongoing governance functions.

## Practical success test

A competent adopter should be able to construct a bounded framework package from the published v1.0.0 material, identify unresolved decisions explicitly, and explain consequential trust decisions in terms of authority, policy, evidence, assurance and remedy **without private maintainer interpretation**.
